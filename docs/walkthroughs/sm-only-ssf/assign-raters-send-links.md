# Assign Raters and Send Links

## Purpose

Show how Senior Management invites peer raters without exposing survey answers in the operations view.

## Steps

1. Senior Management opens the active cycle workspace.
2. Maria Santos is selected as the leader being reviewed.
3. Rafael Cruz, Anna Mercado, and Paolo Reyes are added as peer raters.
4. Survey360 confirms each rater invitation is active.
5. The completion dashboard shows the invitations before responses arrive.

Three peer raters are invited, and Senior Management can track progress without seeing answers.
