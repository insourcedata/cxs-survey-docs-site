# Create SSF Survey

## Purpose

Create a leadership survey grounded in Critical Core Skills, with one additional competency that reflects East West's digital transformation priorities.

## Steps

1. Senior Management opens Survey Cycles and starts a new draft cycle.
2. The cycle is named East West Leadership 360.
3. The survey goal explains that the review will assess leadership behaviours and produce practical development feedback.
4. Senior Management selects Critical Core Skills such as Decision Making and Developing People.
5. Senior Management adds Digital Transformation Leadership as a custom competency for this cycle.

The survey workspace shows the selected skills, the custom competency, and the cycle status.
