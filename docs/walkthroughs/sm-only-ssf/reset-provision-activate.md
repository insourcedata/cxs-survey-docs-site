# Reset, Provision, and Activate

## Purpose

Create a fresh tenant and land in the Senior Management workspace.

## Steps

1. Reset the stack and seed the platform admin.
2. Sign in at `/platform/sign-in` with `admin@survey360.local`.
3. Provision a unique East West organization with industry `Technology`.
4. Use a unique Senior Management email such as `sm-<timestamp>@eastwest.local`.
5. Open the activation email from MailHog and activate the organization.
6. Request the tenant OTP, sign in as Senior Management, and open `/tenant/survey-cycles`.

## Expected result

The organization is active, Senior Management is signed in, and the tenant starts with no survey cycles.
