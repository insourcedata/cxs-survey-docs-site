# Prepare the Organization

## Purpose

Show how the organization is prepared before Senior Management starts the leadership review.

## Steps

1. The platform administrator opens the organizations dashboard.
2. The administrator creates the East West organization and enters the Senior Management email address.
3. Survey360 confirms the organization record.
4. Senior Management activates the organization from the invitation email.
5. Senior Management signs in with an email code and lands in the Survey Cycles workspace.
